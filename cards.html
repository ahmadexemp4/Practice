Twitter

Twitter, officially rebranded as X, is a major social media and microblogging platform where users interact through short text updates, photos, and videos.

Instagram

Instagram, owned by Meta Platforms, is a premier photo and video-sharing social network that shapes modern digital culture and visual media.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp, owned by Meta Platforms, is the world’s most popular instant messaging and voice-over-IP service, connecting over two billion users globally.

Facebook

Facebook, launched by Mark Zuckerberg and his college roommates in 2004, is one of the most influential social networking platforms in history.

Threads

Threads is a microblogging and social networking app developed by Meta Platforms as a direct competitor to X (formerly Twitter). Built by the Instagram team.
